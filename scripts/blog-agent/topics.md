# Blog topics

The blog agent (`AGENT.md`) takes from this list: first a seasonal topic whose window contains today, then the evergreen ones in order. A topic is done once a live post has its slug or answers the same question. Edit freely: add, remove, reorder. Windows are month-day, inclusive, New York time.

Already live (written by hand, November 2026): when to turn sprinklers on and off, dry well vs French drain, pavers vs concrete vs natural stone patios, Brookhaven shed rules and shed bases. Don't repeat them; link them where they help.

Each topic names the service it supports (`service`), what to cover, and what must be verified on an official page before it goes in.

## Seasonal

- **fall-leaf-cleanup-long-island** (service `spring-and-fall-cleanup`), window 10-15 to 12-10. Leaves on the lawn: why a thick layer smothers grass, mulching with the mower versus removing, beds and gutters, when to do the last cleanup. How leaves are collected in the Town of Brookhaven and in the Village of Patchogue (they differ; verify this year's calendars and bag rules on brookhavenny.gov and patchoguevillage.gov). Links: cleanup service page.
- **prepare-shrubs-for-winter-long-island** (service `planting-and-mulch`), window 11-01 to 12-20. Getting shrubs and young trees through winter: watering before the ground freezes, mulch, burlap screens for evergreens exposed to wind or road salt, deer, and which shrubs not to prune in fall (verify with Cornell Cooperative Extension). Links: hedge trimming service page.
- **road-salt-damage-lawn-and-shrubs** (service `lawn-care`), window 12-01 to 02-28. Salt damage along driveways, walks and roads: what it looks like, where to pile snow, gentler deicers and how to use less (verify on Cornell or DEC pages), repairing damaged lawn edges in spring. Links: lawn care, spring cleanup.
- **pavers-in-winter-snow-and-ice** (service `hardscape`), window 12-01 to 02-28. Paver patios and walkways in winter: shoveling and plowing without catching edges, deicers and pavers (tell readers to check their paver maker's guidance; don't name products), what to look at in spring (heaved edges, joint sand). Links: hardscape service page, patio materials post.
- **spring-yard-cleanup-checklist** (service `spring-and-fall-cleanup`), window 03-01 to 04-30. What a spring cleanup covers and in what order: debris and leaves, cutting back perennials and grasses, edging beds, fresh mulch, checking the lawn. Links: cleanup service, mulch topic if live.
- **first-mow-and-spring-lawn-care-long-island** (service `lawn-care`), window 03-15 to 05-15. When to start mowing, mower height, not cutting more than a third, why fertilizer waits until April 1 in Suffolk County (verify the county law page). Links: lawn care, cleanup.
- **when-and-how-to-mulch** (service `planting-and-mulch`), window 03-15 to 05-31. Mulch depth, keeping it off stems and trunks (no mulch volcanoes), edging beds, how often to top up (verify with Cornell). Links: planting and mulch service page.
- **how-to-water-your-lawn-in-summer** (service `irrigation`), window 06-01 to 08-15. How much and how often to water, early morning, deep and less often, SCWA's odd/even schedule and no automatic watering from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. (verify on scwa.com), checking sprinkler coverage. Links: irrigation, sprinkler on/off post.
- **when-to-trim-hedges-long-island** (service `hedge-and-shrub-trimming`), window 05-15 to 07-31. Timing for common hedges and shrubs, shaping so the base gets light, which flowering shrubs to trim right after bloom (verify with Cornell). Links: hedge trimming service page.
- **fall-is-for-planting-trees-and-shrubs** (service `planting-and-mulch`), window 09-01 to 10-31. Why early fall works for planting on Long Island, which trees do better in spring (verify with CCE), watering new plantings until the ground freezes. Links: planting and mulch.
- **fall-overseeding-long-island-lawns** (service `lawn-care`), window 08-15 to 09-30. Overseeding thin lawns: the late-summer window (verify with CCE), preparing the soil, watering new seed, mowing after. Links: lawn care.

## Evergreen

- **retaining-wall-basics** (service `hardscape`). When a slope needs a wall, block versus natural stone, drainage behind the wall, why walls lean. Permits: retaining walls appear on the Town of Brookhaven building permit application; tell readers to ask their town or village before building (verify on brookhavenny.gov; state no height limits you haven't read). Links: hardscape, masonry, drainage.
- **downspout-drainage-options** (service `drainage`). Downspout extensions, splash blocks, underground pipe to a dry well, rain barrels; how far water should end up from the foundation. Links: drainage, dry well post.
- **paver-patio-maintenance** (service `hardscape`). Topping up joint sand, weeds and ants, cleaning, whether to seal, fixing a sunken spot. Links: hardscape, patio materials post.
- **common-sprinkler-problems** (service `irrigation`). A zone that won't turn on or off, low pressure, broken or tilted heads, wet spots that point to a leak, controller settings. What a homeowner can check and when to call. Links: irrigation, sprinkler on/off post.
- **when-to-prune-common-shrubs** (service `hedge-and-shrub-trimming`). Hydrangeas, boxwood, forsythia, azaleas and other shrubs common on Long Island: prune on old or new wood (verify with Cornell). Links: hedge trimming.
- **mulch-types-compared** (service `planting-and-mulch`). Shredded hardwood, bark, dyed mulch, stone: how each holds moisture, breaks down and looks. Links: planting and mulch.
- **mowing-height-guide** (service `lawn-care`). Mowing height through the season, sharp blades, leaving clippings ("Don't Bag It" is the Town of Brookhaven's own advice; verify). Links: lawn care.
- **sod-or-seed-for-a-new-lawn** (service `lawn-care`). Sod versus seed: timing (verify the seeding window with CCE), preparation, watering, when you can walk on it. Links: lawn care, our work page.
- **regrading-a-yard-for-drainage** (service `drainage`). Signs the ground slopes the wrong way, what regrading involves, ask about permits before changing grades (verify on official pages). Links: drainage, dry well post.
- **stone-steps-on-a-slope** (service `masonry`). Natural stone versus block steps, comfortable step height and depth, a solid base, lighting. Links: masonry, hardscape.
- **paver-walkway-planning** (service `hardscape`). Width, layout, base, edging, connecting a walkway to a patio or driveway. Links: hardscape, patio materials post.
- **privacy-plantings-long-island** (service `planting-and-mulch`). Evergreen screens such as arborvitae: spacing, sun and soil, watering the first year, deer (verify plant facts with CCE). Links: planting and mulch, hedge trimming.
- **drip-irrigation-for-garden-beds** (service `irrigation`). Drip versus spray for beds and shrubs, adding drip zones to a sprinkler system, winterizing it. Links: irrigation.
- **path-to-a-shed** (service `sheds`). Getting to the shed: gravel, stepping stones or pavers, width for a mower or wheelbarrow, keeping the doorway dry. Links: shed service, shed rules post, hardscape.
